Prototype 3

UPI app with a working scanner

Pick a tier, then tap the big Scan button. The camera reads any real UPI QR code (upi://pay…). No camera? Upload a QR photo or tap a sample.

What’s new vs. prototype 2
  • Real QR scanning with the camera (jsQR)
  • Scan from a gallery image, or try sample QRs
  • QR amount and payee fill the payment flow automatically
  • My QR screen you can scan from another phone
  • Tier rules apply to scans: kids can only pay approved contacts, elders get a warning for unknown payees
  • PhonePe/GPay-style layout
